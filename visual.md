# Wayfare visual direction

## Direction

Combine Linux’s practical clarity, readable hierarchy, and direct controls with Apple’s calm spacing, careful surfaces, and quiet depth. Keep the result recognizably Wayfare: local, dependable, and warm. Do not copy either platform’s logos or branded interface elements.

Use coral for primary actions and key route points, sage for supporting states, and neutral surfaces for the main reading area. Keep destructive feedback distinct from the brand accent. The dark palette follows the device color scheme.

## Surfaces and type

- Use matte, gently layered surfaces with crisp borders and restrained shadows; avoid heavy glass effects.
- Keep the current Inter family for consistent rendering across native and web.
- Use 18–22 px card corners, 12–14 px control corners, and clear spacing between tasks.
- Use route lines and place labels as a subtle travel motif rather than decoration that competes with the form.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Preserve the existing sign-in, account creation, password reset, and email-verification behavior.
- Keep controls visibly focused, readable in both themes, and large enough for touch.
- Respect reduced-motion settings and do not add sign-in options that are not configured in the product.
