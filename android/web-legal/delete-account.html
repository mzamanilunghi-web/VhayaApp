VHAYA · ACCOUNT & DATA ERASURE

Delete Your Vhaya Account

Option 1: Instant Deletion Inside the Vhaya App (Recommended)

  1. Open the Vhaya Android app and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile/account avatar and open Privacy & POPIA.
  3. Tap Delete account or wipe data, then confirm Delete account now.

Your authentication login, saved places, trusted contacts, and local device cache are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Web / Email (Without App Access)

If you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email hlulanigo@gmail.com from the email address registered on your Vhaya account with subject line “Vhaya Account Deletion Request”.

Our team verifies ownership via your registered email and completes erasure within 7 business days.

What Is Deleted vs. Retained:
• Deleted: Firebase authentication login, saved places, trusted contacts, driver online presence, and device cache.
• Retained (Legal Compliance): Completed trip invoices and Paystack/driver payout records may be anonymized and retained for up to 5 years where required under South African tax and transport regulations (POPIA).
