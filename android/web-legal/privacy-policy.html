Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: November 2026 · Contact: hlulanigo@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Details: Name, email address, role (Rider/Driver), and optional beneficiary pickup contact details provided when booking for family members.
  • Foreground Location Data: Precise or coarse GPS coordinates used solely to set pickup/destination pins, estimate fares, match nearby drivers, track active trips, and power route deviation safety alerts. Background driver location updates run only while a driver chooses to stay Online or during an active trip via an explicit foreground notification.
  • Payments & Wallet Ledger: Card top-ups and payouts are processed via encrypted HTTPS with Paystack. Vhaya stores transaction references and minor-unit ledger balances, never raw card CVVs.
  • Driver Compliance Records: Vehicle make, colour, SA registration number, licence expiry, and operator compliance documents submitted by drivers.

2. How We Use & Share Data

Personal information is used only to operate Vhaya ride requests, driver dispatch, pickup PIN safety verification, support dispute resolution, and statutory tax/transport record keeping under South African law (POPIA).

Vhaya does not sell personal data or show third-party advertising.

3. Data Retention & Security

  • Saved places and trusted emergency contacts stay until edited or deleted by you.
  • Trip, wallet ledger, and support records are retained for 5 years for South African financial/tax compliance.
  • All API and authentication traffic uses TLS/HTTPS encryption.

4. Your Rights (Access, Export & Deletion)

Inside the Vhaya app under Account → Privacy & POPIA, you can toggle location consent, export a complete JSON copy of your records, or permanently delete your account immediately. You may also request deletion anytime via /delete-account or by emailing hlulanigo@gmail.com.
