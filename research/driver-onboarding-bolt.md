# Bolt driver onboarding research

**Reviewed:** 12 November 2026  
**Market context:** South Africa; Wayfare's current launch-market draft is Polokwane.

## What Bolt publicly describes

Bolt's South African signup page starts with a short registration (email, phone,
city and licence details), then directs applicants to document submission and
vehicle setup. It also offers a vehicle-partner route for applicants who do not
own a car. Its South African driver guide lists a valid PrDP, a clear criminal
record check, a profile photo and a cARscan inspection report. The operating
licence guide explains that licences apply per vehicle and that requirements
and processing vary by province.

Bolt also shows a pending-review state and tells drivers when a document needs
to be resubmitted. This keeps account creation separate from approval to drive.

## Wayfare comparison and recommended approach

Wayfare already has staff-controlled driver approval and four compliance
categories: identity, driver licence, vehicle registration and operating
permit. Its current applicant screen asks for vehicle and plate photos, then
hands the person off to their device's email app. That is unreliable across
web and mobile, does not provide an in-app document checklist, and does not
make uploaded evidence available to the staff review screen.

Use the same useful sequence without copying Bolt's brand or exact screens:

1. Collect only the missing contact and service-area details; use the signed-in
   account's existing name and email.
2. Capture vehicle details and explain how to save progress when a vehicle is
   not ready. Do not imply that Wayfare has a rental partner until one exists.
3. Let applicants upload the four documents Wayfare already reviews, directly
   into private storage. State why the files are collected and who can access
   them.
4. Show a persistent application status, received-document list and actionable
   staff feedback. Keep driver access disabled until staff complete their
   checks.

Bolt's extra items (PrDP, criminal-record check and vehicle inspection) are
useful prompts for Wayfare's local checklist, not automatically Wayfare's legal
or operational policy. Confirm which are required in Polokwane and establish
a privacy notice and retention period before collecting documents from public
users. Uploading a file does not verify its contents.
