## Page Summary

outlined\_flag

- The Google Maps Platform team is actively enhancing global coverage for the Routes API.

- Coverage details for the Routes API are provided on a country-by-country basis, including specifics for two-wheeled vehicles and toll calculations.

- Dependent territories with their own CLDR code, excluding those explicitly mentioned, are not supported.


The Google Maps Platform team is constantly working to improve international
coverage for our API services. The following list shows the latest coverage
details, on a country-by-country basis, for the Routes API.

Here are the coverage details for these features of the Routes API:

- [Countries and regions supported for two-wheeled vehicles](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/routes/coverage-two-wheeled)

- [Toll calculation support](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/routes/reference/rest/v2/RouteModifiers#tollpass)


Dependent territories that have their own CLDR code (see
[chart](https://unicode-org.github.io/cldr-staging/charts/latest/supplemental/territory_information.html)
for details), such as U.S. Virgin Islands (VI), unless listed above, are not
supported.
